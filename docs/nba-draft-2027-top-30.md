## Ranking source and method

The initial board uses the public **Ultimate Big Board 2027 consensus** snapshot dated 2026-11-13. Its page reports 14 eligible ranking sources (12 mock drafts and 2 player big boards), 14 analysts across 13 publications, and 111 unique tracked prospects. The order below follows its weighted-average projected selection, not a claim about the eventual draft order.

Source: https://ultimatebigboard.com/nba/2027/

## Data rules

- Keep this board separate from the verified Italian NCAA directory.
- Nationality is not inferred from a player's name, school, or club.
- Preserve the snapshot date and source URL with every refresh.
- Re-rank from the source's latest consensus rather than silently editing prior snapshots.
- A player can appear here even when they are not NCAA-eligible or do not play in Division I.
- Do not attach NCAA season statistics to prospects unless the player has a verified 2026-27 NCAA record.

## Refresh policy

Refresh the consensus snapshot periodically during the 2026-27 season and before the 2027 NBA Draft. Retain dated snapshots so movement can be tracked without overwriting history.
